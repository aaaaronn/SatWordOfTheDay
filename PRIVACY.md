# Privacy Policy — SAT Daily Word

Last updated: Nov 3, 2025

### 1. Overview
SAT Daily Word is designed to be simple and private. We do not collect, store, or share your personal information.

### 2. Data we do not collect
• No name, email, or account information
• No location, contacts, photos, or other device data
• No in‑app analytics or advertising identifiers
• No crash reporting tied to your identity

### 3. How the app works
• The app shows a daily vocabulary word from data bundled with the app.
• The Home Screen widget displays the same word. No data is sent to our servers.

### 4. Third‑party services
We do not use third‑party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking.

### 5. Children’s privacy
SatWordOfTheDay is suitable for students and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

### 6. Contact
If you have questions, contact us at the: [github issues page](https://github.com/aaaaronn/SatWordOfTheDay/issues)

### 7. Changes to this policy
If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this URL.
